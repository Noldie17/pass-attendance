## Notes
- Day grid: columns F to AI hold school days; row 6 = day of month, row 7 = weekday letter. Weekend dates are skipped, so row 6 numbers jump (e.g. 6 then 9).
- Column T rows 8-14 contain the vertical word HOLIDAY (letters H,O,L,I,D,A,Y), meaning that column is a holiday.
- Learner attendance cells (rows 8-22 and 24-37, day columns) are blank in this sample: blank = present.
- Cells shown as =... are formulas (with cached result). Cells in totals columns AM to AP of learner rows show plain numbers in this sample.

## More notes
- Worksheet 1 (named "... (2)") is FEBRUARY; worksheet 2 is MARCH (row 3, cell AA3). Always read the month from "Report for the Month of", never from the sheet name or position.
- A day can span two merged columns (for example F6:G6 holds day 2); write attendance marks into the leftmost cell of the merged range.
- Totals columns: AM7 "ABSENT" and AO7 "PRESENT" (each a merged pair) hold plain numbers per learner in this sample (for example AM8=0, AO8=19, where 19 is the number of school days in the month), so PASS must recompute them. Row 39 AO39=551 is the sum of present days.

## PASS export assumptions
- A saved attendance row with status Absent becomes `x`; Late becomes `L` and receives a light gray fill; Present is blank. A learner with no saved attendance row for the selected date is treated as absent.
- School days are day-number cells in row 6, counting only the leftmost cell of each merged day range. A day column containing the vertical `HOLIDAY` marker is excluded from school-day totals and cannot be selected for export.
- Per-learner monthly totals count `x` as absent; blank and `L` are present. Monthly totals include only active PASS learners matched by normalized name.
- The male/female/combined daily totals are updated for the chosen day from the active PASS roster. Summary registration counts and attendance averages/percentages are recalculated from active learners; the “Enrolment as of (1st Friday of June)” row is treated as a historical baseline and is left unchanged.
- Inactive or unmatched rows already in a teacher's sheet are not removed or rewritten. PASS sets `fullCalcOnLoad` so Excel recalculates formulas when opened. These assumptions are exercised against the anonymized sample fixture; other SF2 template variants still need field validation.
